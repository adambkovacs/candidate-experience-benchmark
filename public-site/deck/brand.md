# Brand

## Name and what we do
AI Enablement Academy (aienablement.academy). This deck is Adam Kovacs' talk "Do models like Jev get it right when correctness is business-critical?", built on a benchmark of 60 synthetic candidate-feedback reviews and four decisions per review.

## Audience
Builders and technical leads deciding whether a fast decision model is safe to put in a business-critical workflow. They watch a screen share over Zoom on a TV, at 1920x1080 or larger.

## What we promote (with the real link)
The benchmark and its saved evidence: https://github.com/adambkovacs/candidate-experience-benchmark. Published report URL: ASK ME.

## The feeling
Technical, calm, premium. Evidence first: every number on screen names its source. Go all out on motion, but premium, not a party.

## Assets
Colours (from the AEA design system tokens, not sampled from images):
- Ink Navy `#1a2332` (ground), Paper `#fbfcfe` (text), Primary Royal Blue `#1565c0`.
- Spectrum ladder, Foundations to Native: `#7e57c2`, `#5c6bc0`, `#29b6f6`, `#66bb6a`, `#fbc02d`, `#f57c00`, `#e53935`.

Fonts (self-hosted in `deck/fonts/`): Archivo 700 to 900 for display, IBM Plex Sans for body, IBM Plex Mono for eyebrows, numbers and code.

Logo files (in `deck/assets/`, never redrawn):
- `aea-logo-horizontal-transparent-white.svg` over dark stages and scenes.
- `aea-logo-horizontal-dark.svg` where the navy plate is wanted.
- `aea-icon-transparent.svg`, the ladder mark, used as a motif and by the particle-mark close.

Presenter photo: `deck/assets/adam.jpg`. QR code: `deck/assets/qr-benchmark.svg`.

## Never
- Spectrum colours as a flat or gradient background. They are for rails, badges, the ladder mark and the progress bar.
- A number that is rounded, invented or unbound. Every figure resolves from a feed and is checked by `verify/numbers.mjs`.
- A replay presented as a live call. The live-replay panel always carries its "Replay of saved answers" badge.
