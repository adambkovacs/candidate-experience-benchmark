# OpenJev generated repeat: two prompt passes

The [fresh v2 plan](../results/repeatability-v1/openjev-generated-fresh-v2/manifest.json) schedules separate generated-off and generated-on P0/P1/P2 triplets. Generated-off has closed both full P0/P1/P2 passes. The [source-bound report](../public-site/openjev-generated-repeats.json) verifies smoke inspections, stage receipts and 60-review development completions for [fresh1 P0](../results/repeatability-v1/openjev-generated-fresh-v2/generated-off/fresh1/P0/development.completion.json), [P1](../results/repeatability-v1/openjev-generated-fresh-v2/generated-off/fresh1/P1/development.completion.json), [P2](../results/repeatability-v1/openjev-generated-fresh-v2/generated-off/fresh1/P2/development.completion.json), and [fresh2 P0](../results/repeatability-v1/openjev-generated-fresh-v2/generated-off/fresh2/P0/development.completion.json), [P1](../results/repeatability-v1/openjev-generated-fresh-v2/generated-off/fresh2/P1/development.completion.json) and [P2](../results/repeatability-v1/openjev-generated-fresh-v2/generated-off/fresh2/P2/development.completion.json). Historical generated observations are excluded from these fresh passes. The unexecuted v1 plan had a wire-body hash defect; v2 uses the frozen composed request bytes.

Each first-pass phase saved **60/60** responses. P0 and P1 each had 53 valid predictions and seven `invalid_output` results; P2 had 56 valid and four invalid. P0's invalid IDs were DEV-001, DEV-012, DEV-017, DEV-018, DEV-028, DEV-029 and DEV-058; P1's were DEV-004, DEV-009, DEV-024, DEV-040, DEV-043, DEV-048 and DEV-056; P2's were DEV-006, DEV-010, DEV-029 and DEV-044. All returned a `stop` finish reason. P0/P1 invalid messages did not parse as JSON. In P2, two were non-JSON, one had an out-of-vocabulary sentiment value, and one misspelled the follow-up key. Every invalid position remains in the fixed denominator; counting only valid outputs would change the question being measured.

P0 and P1 matched all four provisional reference fields on **48/60** reviews; P2 matched **51/60**. Field agreement for sentiment/follow-up/serious concern/testimonial was 52/52/52/51 for P0, 51/52/51/53 for P1, and 55/55/55/54 for P2, each out of 60. Invalid positions account for part of these differences. P0 and P1 have 46 shared-valid reviews, with three changed four-field answers: one serious-concern and two testimonial decisions. P0 and P2 have 50 shared-valid reviews and two changed serious-concern decisions. P1 and P2 have 49 shared-valid reviews and five changed four-field answers: one sentiment, two serious-concern and two testimonial decisions. Follow-up predictions did not change in any pair's shared-valid set. These paired prediction counts and the fixed-60 accuracy scores answer different questions.

Among fresh1 P0's valid outputs, one insufficient-information sentiment reference was marked neutral, one follow-up-yes reference was marked no, one insufficient-information serious-concern reference was marked yes, and two testimonial-yes references were marked no. Among fresh1 P1's valid outputs, two sentiment, one follow-up and two serious-concern decisions missed the reference; all valid testimonial decisions matched it. Among fresh1 P2's valid outputs, one insufficient-information sentiment case was marked neutral, one follow-up-yes case was marked no, one insufficient-information serious-concern case was marked no, and one reference-negative and one reference-positive testimonial case were reversed. These are provisional AI-reviewed labels, not independently adjudicated human ground truth.

Fresh2/P1 also saved 60 responses: 53 valid and seven invalid, with **50/60** all-field agreement. Its field scores were 51/60 sentiment, 53/60 follow-up, 51/60 serious concern and 53/60 testimonial potential. Only DEV-004 was invalid in both P1 passes. Six reviews changed from valid to invalid, and six changed from invalid to valid. Among the 47 reviews valid in both, four changed at least one classification: two sentiment and three serious-concern decisions, with DEV-022 changing both fields. Follow-up and testimonial predictions did not change on those 47. Shared-valid all-field agreement moved from 43/47 to 44/47; the fixed-60 score moved from 48/60 to 50/60 as validity also changed. DEV-006 and DEV-018 became correct on shared-valid reviews, while DEV-030 became incorrect; DEV-022 changed classes but remained incorrect overall. The third P1 pass is still required for a three-pass range.

Fresh2/P2 saved 60 responses: 57 valid and three invalid at DEV-021, DEV-034 and DEV-035. It scored **54/60** all-field agreement, versus 51/60 in fresh1/P2. Its field scores were 56/60 sentiment, 56/60 follow-up, 56/60 serious concern and 57/60 testimonial potential. The two P2 invalid sets do not overlap: four positions changed from invalid to valid, and three from valid to invalid. Among 53 shared-valid reviews, only DEV-028 and DEV-053 changed a prediction, both for testimonial potential. DEV-028 moved from yes to the reference no; DEV-053 moved from no to the reference yes. Shared-valid all-field agreement rose from 48/53 to 50/53. The fixed-60 three-match gain also reflects changed validity, so it is not a three-review classification gain on a constant set. A third P2 pass is still required.

Fresh2/P0 saved 60 responses: 55 valid and five invalid at DEV-001, DEV-002, DEV-015, DEV-016 and DEV-036. It scored **51/60** all-field agreement, versus 48/60 in fresh1/P0. Its field scores were 54/60 for each of sentiment, follow-up, serious concern and testimonial potential. Only DEV-001 was invalid in both P0 passes; six positions changed from invalid to valid and four from valid to invalid. Among 49 shared-valid reviews, only DEV-010 changed a prediction: testimonial potential moved from no to the reference yes. Shared-valid all-field agreement rose from 44/49 to 45/49. The fixed-60 three-match gain also reflects validity turnover.

Each pass now has P0/P1/P2 prompt variants. In fresh2, P1 scored one fewer and P2 three more all-field matches than P0 on the fixed 60 positions; among shared-valid reviews P0-to-P1 and P0-to-P2 each changed three four-field answers. These prompt comparisons and fresh1-to-fresh2 repeat changes remain descriptive. Generated-off is **6/9** and generated-on **0/9** closed phases; fresh3/P2 smoke is closed, but its development has no terminal score here. Third development passes are still required for matched-three repeat ranges. The completed native fixed, adaptive and thinking P0 triplets are separate configurations and do not fill generated phases.

The 60 client HTTP requests took **476.18**, **482.92** and **689.61 seconds** for fresh1 P0/P1/P2, then **428.74**, **490.44** and **663.20 seconds** for fresh2 P0/P1/P2. Returned input/output usage was 100,168/2,172; 110,968/2,645; 166,828/2,764; 100,168/2,183; 110,968/2,626; and 166,828/2,750 tokens, respectively. These are observed request times and reported tokens, not isolated inference time. Model loading was not included in those request sums, and attributable local cost was not measured. The frozen manifest records configured runtime and asset hashes; its requested reasoning setting is not proof of effective reasoning in the loaded model.
