The [full-P0 findings](CLEF_FINDINGS_2026-10-02.md) report 132,694 input tokens per model and list-price estimates of $0.03184656 and $0.01194246, but no provider USD charge was returned. Those estimates cannot release the full-context holds. Cloudflare says its [Billable Usage dashboard](https://developers.cloudflare.com/billing/manage/billable-usage/) draws from the invoicing system, shows daily usage charges and requires Billing Read on a pay-as-you-go account. Its [billing-history API](https://developers.cloudflare.com/api/resources/billing/subresources/history/methods/list/) also requires Billing Read. Read-only connected-app calls to account billing history and billable usage both returned `10000: Authentication error`; no charge rows were obtained. The documented account billing-usage API's listed metrics do not include Workers AI, and AI Gateway usage history would not establish charges for these direct Workers AI calls. An account-level bill or dashboard row must be tied to this cohort and separated from other account activity before any hold is revised. Until then actual USD charges remain unknown.

The next implementation step is a separately versioned smoke-and-full controller that reads the proposed manifest, checks that a chosen stage has no existing claim or attempts, creates an independently reviewed grant, and reserves both Cloudflare and overall authority under locks before dispatch. It must retain the prior P0 evidence unchanged, capture the connected-app result and raw/parsed records durably, stop on uncertain outcome, and score only after a terminal stage. No continuation request has been sent by this proposal.
