Fifteen of the 19 native specialist IDs therefore have saved 60-position P0 outputs, including composite or fold-based outputs whose first-pass eligibility needs separate review. Three original Laya IDs have no inference, and direct AnyJev L1 has no fresh native inference. The TypeSafe native repeat work is already reported separately. None of these counts includes rules, SalesRLAgent, or generated controls.

Four **generated controls** have different dispositions: `openjev-generated-off`, `openjev-generated-on`, `semif-generated-bf16`, and `anyjev-qwen06-generated-control` have P0/P1/P2 prompt conditions in [roster accounting](MVP_ROSTER_ACCOUNTING.md). They are not the native readouts above. OpenJev and AnyJev have 60 accounted positions per condition, including intrinsic invalid outputs; they still need exact first-pass protocol audits before repeat admission. SemIf generated P2 has 59 saved responses, 57 valid, two invalid, and DEV-033 started with unknown outcome after the [interruption and continuation](../results/prompt-comparison-v1-2026-09-24/semif-generated-exact/P2-continuation-v1/reconciliation.json). That P2 cannot be silently treated as a complete clean first pass or replayed; a declared fresh matched series is the available route if its original triple fails eligibility.

Repeated native calls **can** measure observed output variation under pinned controls on these 60 records. They cannot prove a provider or model is intrinsically deterministic, turn repeated records into new samples, or measure prompt P1/P2 effects where the native interface has no such prompt. For any remaining native P0 repeat, first bind the actual original request/operation units, attempts, raw outputs, ordered IDs, artifact hash, runtime, hardware, cache, options and failure policy. Make an explicit first-pass eligibility decision for fixed OpenJev, Alex 4B and every fold-based configuration. Then freeze the exact native schedule and seed policy; inspect a three-record smoke before each admitted 60-record pass, serialize local GPU use, preserve every invalid and unknown position, and report flips on shared-valid IDs alongside 60-position validity. A failed historical request remains in the historical pass. No extra paid TypeSafe call may proceed without the shared-ledger check. These are implementation and evidence gates under the existing repeat objective, not an approval to change the task or add models.
