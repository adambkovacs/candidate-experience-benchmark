# Qwen3.8 27B free-route fresh-series preparation

Two separate, unexecuted configurations are prepared: medium and xhigh reasoning on `qwen/qwen3.8-27b:free` through OpenRouter's `modelrun/fp4` endpoint. Each has fresh1, fresh2, and fresh3 passes in P0/P1/P2, P1/P2/P0, and P2/P0/P1 order. Every condition schedules three smoke requests and 60 development requests, for 567 calls per configuration. The historical and separately frozen paid DeepInfra/bf16 medium and xhigh rows remain pending; the free route does not fulfill them.

The planner reads the six immutable paid input-only manifests by pinned SHA-256. It independently checks all 60 ordered requests in each condition against public input-only feedback, prompt text, JSON schema, saved request hashes, and the old effort and route controls. It does not need the old private provider-error bodies to prepare or verify the new requests. Only model ID, provider-only route, and zero maximum prices change. Temperature 0, max output 4096, strict JSON-schema response format, `reasoning.enabled`, the requested effort, no explicit seed, and the exact messages remain. Reference labels and old predictions never enter a request.

The [public endpoint record](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/qwen/qwen3.8-27b:free/endpoints), checked 2026-09-29, lists one ModelRun `modelrun/fp4` endpoint, status 0, context 262144, zero prompt and completion prices, and `structured_outputs`, `reasoning`, and `reasoning_effort` support. The model catalog advertises both medium and xhigh efforts. The endpoint does not list `response_format` by that name; its `structured_outputs` capability is the relevant OpenRouter metadata, and raw three-record smoke acceptance remains required before any 60-record phase. The runner rechecks the live model and endpoint before loading a credential. It selects only ModelRun, disables fallback, requires supported parameters, sets `max_price` to zero for prompt, completion, request, and image, and stops on any positive returned cost. Missing returned cost is recorded as unobserved rather than measured zero. The [free-variant documentation](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/model-variants/free) treats `:free` as its own route and pricing entry.

The same project-wide private quota ledger used by the Gemma free candidate admits entire stages against the authenticated `/key` daily remaining count, known rolling-24-hour project calls, and at most 20 starts per rolling 60 seconds. The account's free-model counter and higher tier were verified by read-only requests, without publishing values. Activity by other account users can change the counter after admission. A 429 stops the stage with the started position retained and no retry. The ledger is 0600 under a 0700 directory outside Git. Neither free configuration receives a child partition from the project's $10 paid ledger; a paid fallback or nonzero listed price fails the zero-cost route gate. [OpenRouter's limits documentation](https://openrouter.ai/docs/api_reference/limits) describes the daily counter and free-model rate rules.

The executor loads a private copy of the already reviewed Gemma free evidence lifecycle for one fixed Qwen configuration per process. It does not change that module's globals or the paid Qwen controller. Each configuration has a separate private evidence root and exclusive lock. A phase-specific private root-review receipt must bind configuration, stage, the wrapper and lifecycle source hashes, tests hash, all three plan hashes, and evidence root. The root reviews and inspects all three raw smoke responses before development; the inspection binds closed raw, attempt, wire, and journal hashes. Bounded raw HTTP bytes are saved before parsing. An interrupted or failed phase is not replayed. Raw bodies may contain account identifiers and cannot be published without a separate allowlisted projection.

This preparation made no inference call, budget allocation, or provider request. Offline tests and manifest verification must pass after independent review. Live dispatch needs fresh zero-price route and account quota checks, a private stage receipt, and the smoke inspection gate. A public endpoint can still reject the strict schema despite its advertised structured-output capability; such a 404 or 429 is retained as a failed attempt and stops the series.
