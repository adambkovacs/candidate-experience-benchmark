# Reader audit — 1 October 2026

This is a source-level review for recruiting, HR and business readers. It covers the public site as it stood during this pass, including the concurrent reader-story revision. The site describes 60 fictional comments and provisional AI-authored reference answers; it does not establish performance with real candidates. The labels below use the repository review rubric: **BLOCKING** requires a reachable failure of the page's feature or a material regression; **RESIDUAL** identifies a confirmed clarity issue that does not meet that threshold. I found no confirmed blocking issue in this bounded review. Browser behavior beyond the publisher's reported checks was not independently measured here.

1. **RESIDUAL — Put the reference dispute beside the first score.** The hero warns that the reference answers are provisional, and the Jev chapter says a correction has been proposed, but the specific case and unresolved review are down in the findings section. A reader scanning 54/60 could still treat every disagreement as a model mistake. Keep the existing 54/60 score and add a short link from the Jev chapter to the disputed reference status. Sources: [hero and Jev chapter](../public-site/index.html), [findings rendering](../public-site/findings.js). Reason: the qualification is present, so this is a placement issue rather than a false score.

2. **RESIDUAL — Define the 39 comparisons at first encounter.** The 21/39 prompt result counts paired *first-pass setup* comparisons, not comments or candidates; its chart also cautions that the prompt alone is not proven causal. The revised glance card now names paired first-pass setups, and the chapter names model, route and settings. Keep both qualifications whenever this number is reused. Sources: [glance card and prompt chapter](../public-site/index.html), [prompt comparison data](../public-site/findings-provider-errors-v1.json). Reason: the current copy gives the denominator and limitation, with a remaining risk for readers who only see the large numeral.

3. **RESIDUAL — Distinguish a totals picture from case positions.** The Jev 60-cell graphic groups its six differing cells at the end for a simple total, while the repeat graphic uses actual DEV-001–DEV-060 positions. The revised Jev caption now says the six circles are grouped, and the repeat caption states its order. Preserve that distinction in accessible descriptions and any future animation. Sources: [chart markup and captions](../public-site/index.html), [grid construction](../public-site/reader.js). Reason: captions now prevent a factual misreading for attentive readers, but the two similar graphics still demand care.

4. **RESIDUAL — Keep saved results separate from usable answers at the rankings.** A complete run means all 60 results were saved, even if some answers missed the required format. The [ranking and detail code](../public-site/app.js) now puts the required-format count next to the score and the saved count in the detail badge; the [ranking explanation](../public-site/index.html) retains the completion definition. Keep unavailable counts as unavailable. Reason: the change improves the immediate reading of a row, though a reader can still overlook the smaller format count.

5. **RESIDUAL — Offer a short repeat summary while retaining the limits.** The original repeat lead combined pass counts, failures, model distinctions, timing and cost qualifications in one long paragraph before the results. The [repeat view](../public-site/repeats.js) now shows a short series summary and places that full text inside a native “Study details and measurement limits” disclosure. It also spells out that change comparisons include only comments whose answers met the required format in both runs, or all three passes. Reason: the limits remain available, but readers must deliberately open them to interpret edge cases.

The implementation in `app.js` and `repeats.js` changes presentation only. It does not alter source feeds, denominators, scores, request evidence or deep links. The reader-story revision is owned and reviewed separately.
