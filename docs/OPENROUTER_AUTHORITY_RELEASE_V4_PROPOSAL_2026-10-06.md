# OpenRouter-only authority release transition

The [read-only audit](OPENROUTER_HOLD_RELEASE_AUDIT_2026-10-06.md) identified **$4.894540233597** of unused allocation across seven closed OpenRouter-only children. Their child ledgers are sealed and the master has a reconciliation for each. Their known charges and **$0.11345920 of unknown-cost upper bounds** must remain accounted for. The amount is still held in the [authority ledger](../results/postapproval-paid-work-2026-10-02.jsonl); this proposal has not released it.

The new [v4 release adapter](../scripts/openrouter_authority_release_v4.py) is separate from the frozen [v3 authority module](../scripts/postapproval_authority_v3.py). It derives one proposed release from an exact held budget manifest, master allocation and reconciliation, and sealed child. It checks the child SHA-256, matches every reservation to a known settlement or retained unknown bound, and requires the proposed amount to equal only the unused allocation. Its scanner preserves the original authority history and reviewed amendment, then accounts for v3 holds and v4 releases by funding pool. An append requires an exact independent root receipt and unchanged authority and master heads. **The first release additionally requires every existing OpenRouter-only held child to be closed and reconciled.** Later releases require their own target child to be closed, while an unrelated new v4 child may remain active. [Focused tests](../tests/test_openrouter_authority_release_v4.py) use isolated authority, master, and child ledgers to check unknown bounds, stale and duplicate releases, first-transition refusal, and a later release alongside an active new child.

**The first transition cannot be applied while any v3 consumer is live.** At the audited checkpoint, [Qwen remaining](../results/repeatability-v1/qwen36-on-hosted-authority-v3-v2/remaining-hosted-v1), [DeepSeek high remaining-seven](../results/repeatability-v1/deepseek-high-remaining7-price-v1), and [DeepSeek low remaining-six](../results/repeatability-v1/deepseek-low-remaining6-price-v2) children were unreconciled. Their frozen runners call `postapproval_authority_v3._scan` or `read_authority`; that module rejects a v4 release event. The adapter's first-release child gate blocks an append until those children close, and the root review receipt must separately attest that no v3 consumer remains in flight. Do not edit those bound runner sources to force an early transition. [Current v3 reader](../scripts/postapproval_authority_v3.py), [v4 adapter](../scripts/openrouter_authority_release_v4.py).

After those children terminate and are reconciled, the coordinated path is:

1. Stop admitting new work through v3 and confirm no v3 runner can still read the authority ledger. Recheck the authority and master heads and all held child closures. Review the v4 adapter and its test results against those current bytes.
2. Generate a fresh `plan` for **one** closed hold using `python3 scripts/openrouter_authority_release_v4.py plan --hold-id HOLD_ID --release-id UNIQUE_ID > plan.json`. Create its exact `review-template` with `--plan-file plan.json`, inspect the evidence, and have root fill `approved`, `independent_review`, `reviewer`, and `v3_consumers_quiesced`. A stale plan or mismatched receipt fails before append.
3. Append that single reviewed event with `python3 scripts/openrouter_authority_release_v4.py append --plan-file plan.json --review-file review.json`. Repeat with a **new plan and receipt** at the new authority head for each other closed hold. The writer never auto-approves or batches releases. Verify the v4 scanner and the unchanged child/master cost accounting after each event.
4. Future authority users must import the v4 scanner and v4 `hold_authority` through a **separately versioned, reviewed execution adapter**. The old v3 readers cannot operate after the first release. Recheck provider account credit, master headroom, route, request, and stage receipts before allocating any new child. Releasing an authority hold alone does not provide account credit or authorize inference.

The seven release candidates, in any reviewed order, are the low DEV-051–060 suffix ($0.626319991993), Gemini high repeats ($0.364000), Qwen thinking-on parent ($1.3400876), original DeepSeek high ($0.832188641604), Qwen P1 unsent continuation ($0.3155024), hosted Mistral smoke ($0.70822080), and plain Mistral smoke ($0.70822080). Use the exact hold IDs in the [audit](OPENROUTER_HOLD_RELEASE_AUDIT_2026-10-06.md); do not infer them from these labels. The authority ledger has not been mutated by this proposal.
