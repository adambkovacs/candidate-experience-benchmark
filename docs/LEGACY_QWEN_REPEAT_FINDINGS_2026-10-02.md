Each phase saved 60 of 60 responses and passed the report's raw-response, request-identity, runtime-control and completion-hash checks. **Qwen3 0.6B · SDK · thinking on** and **thinking off** each have all three complete passes of P0, P1 and P2. Qwen3 1.7B SDK thinking-on has closed seven of nine phases: fresh1 P0, P1 and P2, fresh2 P0, P1 and P2, and fresh3 P1. ## Qwen3 0.6B SDK results

The two SDK fresh1/P0 smokes were originally stopped after three attempts because strict parsing found intrinsic fenced-JSON output (two invalid with thinking on; three with thinking off). The separately approved [SDK format successor](../results/repeatability-v1/legacy-qwen-sdk-format-successor-v1/manifest.json) admitted new development phases without repairing or replaying those smokes. The report binds each terminal smoke, successor inspection and receipt, and development completion and raw response. At P0, thinking on saved 60/60 with **27 valid and 33 invalid outputs**; thinking off saved 60/60 with **1 valid and 59 invalid outputs**. Both score **0/60 all-four exact matches** against the frozen provisional key. At P2, thinking on saved 60/60 with **56 valid, 4 invalid, and 3/60 all-four matches**; thinking off saved 60/60 with **1 valid, 59 invalid, and 0/60 all-four matches**. The thinking-on P2 smoke was all valid; the thinking-off P2 smoke stopped with three invalid outputs and had only a successor inspection, which the report binds directly. Invalid outputs remain in the fixed denominator. These fresh1 rows are single-pass observations; the completed repeats are reported below. The [thinking-on P2 completion](../results/repeatability-v1/legacy-qwen-fresh3-v1/qwen3-0.6b-sdk-thinking-on/fresh1/P2/development.completion.json) and [thinking-off P2 completion](../results/repeatability-v1/legacy-qwen-fresh3-v1/qwen3-0.6b-sdk-thinking-off/fresh1/P2/development.completion.json) retain the outcomes.

All three SDK P0 passes are now complete too. Thinking-on valid counts were 27, 31 and 30 of 60; all-four matches were zero each time. Only five reviews were valid in all three passes, and every one changed at least one label. Thinking-off valid counts were 1, 0 and 1, with no shared-valid reviews and zero all-four matches. The unchanged score is not evidence of stable classifications.

All three SDK P1 passes are now complete. With thinking on, valid outputs numbered 53, 55 and 48 of 60, and all-four matches numbered 1, 3 and 2. At least one label changed across the three passes for 36 of 40 reviews valid in all three. With thinking off, valid outputs numbered 4, 1 and 3; every pass had zero all-four matches and no review was valid in all three, so classification stability is unavailable. The unchanged zero score does not demonstrate unchanged classifications.

All three SDK P2 passes are now complete. Thinking-on valid counts were 56, 58 and 58 of 60; all-four matches were 3, 1 and 1. Of 52 reviews valid in all three passes, 47 changed at least one label. Thinking-off valid counts were 1, 4 and 5, with zero all-four matches in every pass and no shared-valid reviews across all three. More valid outputs under the decision-tree prompt did not establish reliable classification: the final thinking-on pass matched sentiment on 37/60 reviews, follow-up on 44/60, serious concern on 21/60, and testimonial potential on 13/60. These are repeated measurements on the same reviews, not additional independent cases.

The final P2 runs began after a separately reviewed operational host rebaseline. The Mac's intervening sleep occurred between stages; the original sleep record was retained, and pre/post checks found no additional sleep during either final phase. Model, prompt, parser and runtime controls were unchanged.

The added instructions improved format compliance in this thinking-on first pass, while complete-label agreement stayed low. The original outputs remain available for a separately declared parsing study if desired.

These counts describe this Qwen3 0.6B artifact through the local HTTP nonthinking route; they are not findings about every Qwen model or a measure of hiring accuracy.

The three fresh passes produce the same four-field prediction for every record within each prompt condition: all nine pairwise comparisons have 0/60 changed answer vectors, and no field changes across all three passes. That is output stability on the *same* 60 synthetic reviews, not independent-case performance. The fixed denominator for scores is 60; change comparisons use shared valid records, which are all 60 here. Historical Qwen predictions remain separate observations and do not fill these fresh cells.

The [plan](../results/repeatability-v1/legacy-qwen-fresh3-v1/manifest.json) fixes each configuration's model artifact, context, output reserve and cache policy; each stage binds its runtime attestation. The HTTP nonthinking route uses an 8,192-token context, 512-token output reserve, temperature zero and no controlled seed. Across its nine development phases, reported usage totals 996,960 tokens and 204.09 seconds of client-observed request time. That time includes runtime and transport overhead, so pure inference and model-load times are unavailable. The local runs have no measured per-request API charge; hardware and electricity costs were not measured. The two other larger SDK configurations have no closed full phases yet.
