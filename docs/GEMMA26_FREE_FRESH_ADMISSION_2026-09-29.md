# Gemma 26B free-route fresh series: offline admission

This is a separate, unexecuted nine-condition study of `google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it:free` through OpenRouter's `google-ai-studio` endpoint. It uses the same 60 inputs, P0/P1/P2 instructions, strict response schema, temperature 0, 4096 output-token limit, and `reasoning.enabled: true` as the frozen Gemma 26B paid-route plan. Each fresh pass has a three-request smoke before its 60-request development stage. The three condition orders are P0/P1/P2, P1/P2/P0, and P2/P0/P1. The planned 27 smokes and 540 development calls total 567. The original DeepInfra/fp8 reasoning-on configuration remains unexecuted at 0/9; this new Google AI Studio route does not complete or replace it.

The [free-variant documentation](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/model-variants/free) treats `:free` as a separate catalog entry with its own pricing and endpoints. The [public endpoint record](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it:free/endpoints), checked on 2026-09-29, showed one Google AI Studio endpoint with zero prompt and completion prices, unknown quantization, context 262144, and 32768 maximum completion tokens. It advertised `reasoning`, `include_reasoning`, and `response_format`, but did not advertise `structured_outputs`. The executor therefore checks the live model and endpoint again, selects only that provider, disables fallback, sets zero maximum prices for prompt, completion, request, and image, and stops on any positive returned cost. An omitted request-fee catalog field is not by itself proof of zero cost: the explicit free variant and zero-price request guard are both required. A missing returned cost remains *not observed*, even when the route's advertised bound is zero. The project's $10 paid ledger receives no allocation; a different or charged route fails admission.

The [OpenRouter limits documentation](https://openrouter.ai/docs/api_reference/limits) describes the free-model daily counter returned by authenticated `GET /key`, as well as the free tier's request-per-minute rule. Read-only checks on this account established the higher 1000/day tier and an integer remaining counter without recording account values. Stage admission rechecks `/key` and `/credits`; it requires a 1000-call tier and at least the full 3 or 60 calls remaining after other outstanding project reservations. The private shared ledger at `/private/tmp/candidate-benchmark-openrouter-free-quota-v1/events.jsonl` atomically reserves whole stages across free-model configurations, caps known project reservations at 1000 per rolling 24 hours, and spaces starts by at least 3.1 seconds while enforcing at most 20 starts per rolling 60 seconds. The ledger is mode 0600 in a 0700 directory and stores only stage IDs, record IDs, timestamps, and counts. Activity by other account users can consume capacity after admission; a 429 stops the stage without retry or replay.

No request has been sent under this plan. Before any stage, an independent reviewer must verify the manifest and controller hashes, current endpoint controls and zero prices, private evidence directory, and a phase-specific private mode-0600 receipt. The receipt schema is `gemma26-free-fresh-root-review-v1` with `approved:true`, exact configuration and `freshN/Px/{smoke|development}` stage, controller and tests SHA-256, all three manifest SHA-256 values, and an absolute private `evidence_root`. It contains no account balance or quota value. Smoke must close with three raw, schema-valid results. A reviewer must inspect their raw responses and record a hash-bound `smoke.inspection.json` before development. The executor claims each phase exclusively, writes a request intent and bounded raw HTTP body before parsing, and never resends a started record. HTTP errors, malformed bodies, a changed route, a positive charge, and unknown outcomes stop the affected stage. Raw provider bodies and private account checks remain outside Git; publication needs a separate allowlisted export.
