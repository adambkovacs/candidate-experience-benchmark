# AnyJev L2: two observed native passes

The historical native L2 pass and the completed second pass produced the same four-field prediction on all 60 development reviews. Each had 60 valid outputs and matched all four provisional reference labels on **13/60** reviews. Field agreement in each pass was sentiment 33/60, follow-up 43/60, serious concern 36/60 and testimonial potential 47/60. The observed pairwise flip denominator is 60, with zero flips in every field. A third pass has a frozen plan but no completed observations, so the three-pass mean, range and across-pass change set remain unavailable.

These are supervised native-head results on the same development records, not an independent estimate of accuracy. For each of five fixed outer folds, L2 fits four heads using the other 48 reviews and their provisional labels, then predicts the 12 held-out reviews. Each pass contains 20 fits and 240 held-out field decisions. The historical pass used a reviewed three-review smoke and then added the other 57 records; repeat 2 used the same staged procedure and reused its own reviewed smoke outputs and heads. The [frozen repeat plan](../results/repeatability-v1/anyjev-l2-cv5-v1/repeat2-plan.json) binds the model, source, runtime, folds and request controls. The label boundary is enforced in code within one process, without operating-system isolation.

The observed agreements hide class-specific errors. On follow-up, repeat 2 labeled 29 of 35 reference-positive reviews “yes” and labeled nine of 24 reference-negative reviews “yes.” On testimonials, it labeled four of nine reference-positive reviews “yes” and five of 50 reference-negative reviews “yes.” The reference labels are AI reviewed and provisional; they are not independent human ground truth.

The [public report](../public-site/anyjev-l2-repeats.json) includes the 60-position scores, confusion counts, class distributions, source hashes and the shared-valid flip comparison. Its repeat-2 evidence binds the [closed wrapper completion](../results/repeatability-v1/anyjev-l2-cv5-v1/repeat2/full.repeat-completion.json), the root-reviewed smoke and full receipts, the smoke inspection, raw native returns, operation journals, projected rows, and all five fold artifacts. Historical and repeat-2 model controls are frozen, but their instrumentation differs: the new wrapper saves raw returns before projection. A third pass is not inferred from that match.

Client record elapsed times sum to 190.99 seconds for the historical pass and 156.44 seconds for repeat 2. Those sums exclude a defensible separate measurement of model load, head fitting and pure inference. Native token totals, isolated inference time and local hardware or electricity cost were not measured and remain null. These L2 results should be compared within their fitted-head procedure, not treated as uncalibrated P0 chat outputs.
