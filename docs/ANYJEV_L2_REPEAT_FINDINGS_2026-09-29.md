# AnyJev L2: three observed native passes

The historical native L2 pass and two completed repeats produced the same four-field prediction on all 60 development reviews. Each had 60 valid outputs and matched all four provisional reference labels on **13/60** reviews. Field agreement in each pass was sentiment 33/60, follow-up 43/60, serious concern 36/60 and testimonial potential 47/60. All three pairwise comparisons have 60 shared valid reviews and zero flips in every field. The three-pass all-field range is 13–13/60, and no review changed its four-field vector. Agreement on this fixed set does not prove identical behavior on other inputs or runtimes.

These are supervised native-head results on the same development records, not an independent estimate of accuracy. For each of five fixed outer folds, L2 fits four heads using the other 48 reviews and their provisional labels, then predicts the 12 held-out reviews. Each pass contains 20 fits and 240 held-out field decisions. Each pass used a reviewed three-review smoke and then added the other 57 records, reusing its own smoke outputs and heads. The separate [repeat-2 plan](../results/repeatability-v1/anyjev-l2-cv5-v1/repeat2-plan.json) and [repeat-3 plan](../results/repeatability-v1/anyjev-l2-cv5-v1/repeat3-plan.json) bind the model, source, runtime, folds and request controls. The label boundary is enforced in code within one process, without operating-system isolation.

The observed agreements hide class-specific errors. On follow-up, repeat 2 labeled 29 of 35 reference-positive reviews “yes” and labeled nine of 24 reference-negative reviews “yes.” On testimonials, it labeled four of nine reference-positive reviews “yes” and five of 50 reference-negative reviews “yes.” The reference labels are AI reviewed and provisional; they are not independent human ground truth.

The [public report](../public-site/anyjev-l2-repeats.json) includes the 60-position scores, confusion counts, class distributions, source hashes, three pairwise comparisons and the three-pass range. Each new pass binds its own [repeat-2](../results/repeatability-v1/anyjev-l2-cv5-v1/repeat2/full.repeat-completion.json) or [repeat-3](../results/repeatability-v1/anyjev-l2-cv5-v1/repeat3/full.repeat-completion.json) closed wrapper completion, root-reviewed smoke and full receipts, smoke inspection, raw native returns, operation journals, projected rows, and all five fold artifacts. Model controls are frozen across the three passes, but historical and repeat instrumentation differ: the new wrapper saves raw returns before projection.

Client record elapsed times sum to 190.99, 156.44 and 150.42 seconds for the historical pass, repeat 2 and repeat 3. Those sums exclude a defensible separate measurement of model load, head fitting and pure inference. Native token totals, isolated inference time and local hardware or electricity cost were not measured and remain null. These L2 results should be compared within their fitted-head procedure, not treated as uncalibrated P0 chat outputs.
