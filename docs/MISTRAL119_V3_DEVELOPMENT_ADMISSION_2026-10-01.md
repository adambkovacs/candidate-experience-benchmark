# Mistral Small 4 fresh1/P0 development preparation, 1 October 2026

The [v3 protocol smoke](../results/repeatability-v1/mistral119-fresh-matched3-v1/v3-protocol-smoke-none-v1/smoke.inspection.json) finished with three valid responses on DEV-002–004. Root inspected the raw responses and recorded a `PASS`; the [sealed child reconciliation](../results/repeatability-v1/mistral119-fresh-matched3-v1/v3-protocol-smoke-none-v1/budget-reconciliation.json) records $0.000337980 in known charges and no unknown charge. This smoke was a separate provider check, with no repeat-pass credit. The five earlier DEV-001 HTTP 429 attempts remain as failed history in the [frozen Mistral plan](../scripts/mistral119_fresh_repeat_study.py).

The new [development adapter](../scripts/mistral119_v3_development.py) prepares **fresh1/P0 only**. It binds the committed smoke inspection to the claim, journal, three attempts, raw responses, parsed responses, and sealed child. It reparses each raw body, checks route and response identity, and requires three valid, known-billed outcomes. It then binds all 60 ordered P0 development requests from the frozen [fresh1 manifest](../results/repeatability-v1/mistral119-fresh-matched3-v1/openrouter-paid-mistral-small4-119b-none/fresh1/manifest.json). Those requests use only the original feedback inputs and labeling policy; reference labels remain outside the request path.

A distinct **$0.25** v3 child is proposed under the $12.38 master. The [frozen full-context bound](../scripts/mistral119_fresh_repeat_study.py) is $0.04177920 per call, so the child can admit a full reserve immediately. The three smoke charges average $0.000112660 each; multiplying that observed average by 60 gives $0.006759600. This is a planning observation, not a price guarantee. The adapter reserves the full bound before every call and stops before dispatch when the child cannot cover the next reserve. It does not demand 60 simultaneous worst-case reservations, which would cost $2.506752 and exceed the proposed child. A root receipt must bind the stage manifest, controller hash, inspected smoke, exact child manifest and cap before any dispatch.

The adapter rechecks the [exact OpenRouter route and pricing](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/mistralai/mistral-small-2603/endpoints) before admission and before each request through the frozen [Mistral live-controls verifier](../scripts/mistral119_fresh_repeat_execution.py). It uses the unchanged `none` reasoning control, payloads and strict classifier. It records request intent and start, bounded private HTTP bytes, billing outcome, parsed result and terminal status durably. Unknown billing retains the full reservation; a known charge above its bound is settled at the reported amount and blocks the child. Provider, route or output failure stops the stage without retry. Completed responses do not authorize another run of the same stage.

Offline preparation is `python3 scripts/mistral119_v3_development.py prepare`; verification is `python3 scripts/mistral119_v3_development.py verify`. The future `run` command needs a separately allocated child and checked `development.root-review.json`, passed as `--budget-manifest` and `--root-review-receipt`. Preparation does not allocate, send, score or claim completion. The [focused tests](../tests/test_mistral119_v3_development.py) exercise the inspection gate, 60-request identity, duplicate refusal, route drift and budget failure paths with a fake transport and ledger. They cannot establish future provider access.

The other eight Mistral `none` phases are outside this adapter. Each needs its own inspected smoke and stage receipt. A generic phase switch would hide those still-open gates, so later phases should be prepared as a separate reviewed extension.
