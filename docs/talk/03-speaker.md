## 4. Why I ran this benchmark (angle, first person for the talk)

- **I have read the pile.** Years of sourcing, recruiting and running talent intelligence at AWS means I know what candidate feedback looks like and how hard it is to read at volume. Classifying it by hand does not scale, and nobody does it consistently.
- **Candidate experience is a hiring-systems problem, and fixing hiring systems is the work I have done since The Talent Foundation (2022).** OPEN Talent Society and DevMatch put me close to the same question from the community and tooling sides: how do you measure whether the process is fair and candidate-focused?
- **Vendors claim accuracy, so I measured it.** Niveus and Amazon taught me to build the measure before trusting the tool. The benchmark compares a specialised classifier against LLMs on the same 60 reviews, with costs, tokens and prompt variants published, so a talent team can decide with evidence. (Basis: P80 "AI is a tool... sometimes AI is not the solution".)
