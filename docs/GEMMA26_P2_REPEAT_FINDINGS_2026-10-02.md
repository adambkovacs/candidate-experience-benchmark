# Gemma 26B thinking-on P2 across three passes

The three P2 passes have fixed-60 all-four agreement scores of **57, 56 and 56** against the same [frozen provisional labels](../data/pilot/proposed_labels.jsonl). The range is one comment. Valid response counts are **59, 60 and 58**: fresh pass 1 retains the DEV-007 service failure, while fresh pass 3 retains service failures at DEV-005 and DEV-006. Failed requests count in each 60-comment score and were not replayed. These are descriptive results from interrupted dispatches, **not a clean matched-three experiment**. The complete counts and source hashes are in the [P2 comparison feed](../public-site/gemma26-p2-repeat-findings.json).

Fresh pass 3 P0 and P1 were never sent; the wider Gemma series has seven of nine scored conditions, not nine.

For a like-for-like answer comparison, **57 comments have valid answers in all three P2 passes**; DEV-005, DEV-006 and DEV-007 are excluded from this paired set. All-four reference matches on those 57 are **56, 55 and 55**. Only two comments change any decision across the three passes:

- **DEV-013:** sentiment changes from `neutral` in pass 1 to `positive` in passes 2 and 3, while its provisional reference is `neutral`. This loses one all-four match between passes 1 and 2; the other three decisions stay the same. - **DEV-059:** follow-up changes from `insufficient_information` in passes 1 and 2 to `no` in pass 3. The provisional reference is `yes`, so neither answer gains a match. The other three decisions stay the same. There are no all-four reference-match gains between these passes on the 57 shared-valid comments. Pass 1 to pass 2 loses DEV-013; pass 2 to pass 3 changes DEV-059's answer without changing its mismatch status. Pairwise comparisons using every answer valid in each *pair* have different denominators: **59** for passes 1–2, **58** for passes 2–3, and **57** for passes 1–3. On the 59 shared by passes 1–2, DEV-006 also changes follow-up from the reference `yes` to `insufficient_information`, losing another match. It is absent from the all-three comparison because pass 3 has a service failure there. The [source-bound feed](../public-site/gemma26-p2-repeat-findings.json) lists every changed ID and each gained or lost field match.

The [reference class balance](../public-site/gemma26-p2-repeat-findings.json) is uneven: 31 of 60 sentiment labels are `negative`, and 50 of 60 testimonial labels are `no`. Among *valid* answers, serious-concern and testimonial decisions match their references in every pass. The remaining usable-answer disagreements are in sentiment and follow-up: pass 1 has one of each, pass 2 has two of each, and pass 3 has one of each. The feed includes a full reference-by-prediction confusion matrix for each field and pass, with unavailable answers in their own column. Class balance and agreement with these provisional labels do not establish accuracy on real candidate feedback.

The first pass combines a stopped prefix and later suffix; the third combines six separately dispatched stage ranges. Their provider failures and unknown-charge evidence remain in the underlying [cutoffs](../public-site/gemma26-postabort-findings.json). No failed request was repaired or retried for this comparison. Request durations are client request-to-record times, including transport and service overhead, and cannot isolate model inference time. The comments are synthetic, and the frozen v0.2 labels were human checked but remain provisional. The offline [builder](../scripts/build_gemma26_p2_repeat_findings.py) validates the prior published feeds, reviewed projection, frozen request IDs and hashes, then scores and compares only structured judgments; its [tests](../tests/test_gemma26_p2_repeat_findings.py) check per-record flips as well as aggregate counts.
