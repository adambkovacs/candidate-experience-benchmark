## Why these two configurations

OpenRouter documents a native [`POST /api/alpha/decisions`](https://openrouter.ai/docs/api/api-reference/alphadecisions/submit-a-decisions-request) endpoint with typed questions, state, provider routing preferences, a returned provider/model, and usage cost. The prepared requests reuse the repository's exact four native Jev `choice` questions, criteria, policy, and input-only development rows. They do not use chat completion or a generated JSON answer. OpenRouter Jev is a **new hosted route for an existing model**, while Kev is a **new model**. The completed smokes are entry checks toward the goal's full model and condition passes; they do not substitute for them.

The public [Jev endpoint listing](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/typesafe/jev-1.13/endpoints) exposed one TypeSafe endpoint, `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917`, with 32,000 context tokens. The public [Kev listing](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/jaredpalmer/kev-4b/endpoints) exposed one SiliconFlow fp8 endpoint, `jaredpalmer/kev-4b-20260924`, with 8,192 context tokens. Both listed `text->decisions`, `$0.000000042` per input token, zero output-token price, and zero discount at preparation. Snapshots are saved beside the manifest. The [Decisions reference](https://openrouter.ai/docs/api/api-reference/alphadecisions/submit-a-decisions-request) shows the versioned Jev response, provider name, four-choice probability shape, and usage cost. These public listings establish a route and catalog tariff, not an invoice quote.

## Bound and gates

The smoke runner reserved the full listed context at the pinned input rate. The endpoint listing had zero output price and no per-request price field; requests set `provider.max_price.request` to zero. The bounds were `$0.001344` per Jev request (`32,000 × $0.000000042`) and `$0.000344064` per Kev request (`8,192 × $0.000000042`): **$0.004032** and **$0.001032192** for three requests, respectively. [OpenRouter's provider routing guide](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/provider-selection) documents `only`, `allow_fallbacks: false`, and `max_price` in USD per million tokens, with missing-price routes rejected. The six observed responses came from the expected providers and versioned models at costs below these bounds. That observation does not independently prove how the alpha router would handle every future control or tariff change; the controller checks live endpoint identity and price before dispatch and returned identity afterward.
