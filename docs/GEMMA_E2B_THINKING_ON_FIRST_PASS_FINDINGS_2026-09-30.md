# Gemma 4 E2B thinking-on: first P0 and P1 passes

The [frozen small-local plan](../results/repeatability-v1/small-local-v1/manifest.json) schedules three separately dispatched development passes under each P0, P1 and P2 prompt. The [source-bound report](../public-site/small-local-repeats.json) contains two closed full phases for the thinking-on configuration: fresh1/P0 and fresh1/P1. The remaining seven phases have no full score in this publication snapshot. These are first-pass observations, not a completed repeatability comparison.

Both phases returned valid decisions for all 60 synthetic reviews. Among those same reviews, the four-field answer changed for **13/60** between P0 and P1. Three reviews gained an all-field match against the [provisional version 0.2 references](../data/pilot/proposed_labels.jsonl) and five lost one, producing the observed 38-to-36 score change. The saved [P0](../results/repeatability-v1/small-local-v1/gemma4-e2b-sdk-thinking-on/fresh1/P0/development.records.jsonl) and [P1](../results/repeatability-v1/small-local-v1/gemma4-e2b-sdk-thinking-on/fresh1/P1/development.records.jsonl) records show seven sentiment changes, one follow-up change, six serious-concern changes and three testimonial changes; a review can change more than one field. These first-pass differences do not show whether either prompt would keep its score in later passes, and they do not isolate a prompt effect.

The table keeps every score on the fixed 60-review denominator. Its seconds sum client-observed request durations and include local workflow overhead; isolated inference duration and model load time are unavailable. The saved local token counts are not hosted billing records. Hardware and electricity cost were not measured, so dollar cost is unavailable. The frozen plan and [reporter](../scripts/build_small_local_repeat_findings.py) preserve one attempt per review and use the provisional references only for offline scoring.

The original P0 and P1 smoke inspections omitted an explicit statement that reference labels were withheld. [Post-run P0](../results/repeatability-v1/small-local-v1/gemma4-e2b-sdk-thinking-on/fresh1/P0/smoke-inspection-reference-attestation.json) and [P1](../results/repeatability-v1/small-local-v1/gemma4-e2b-sdk-thinking-on/fresh1/P1/smoke-inspection-reference-attestation.json) attestations bind the original inspection, plan and smoke hashes without changing the original evidence. The [review note](SMALL_LOCAL_REPEAT_REVIEW_2026-09-28.md) explains this supplement and its limits. The report binds the closed full phases and lists SHA-256 paths for its sources.

The completed [Gemma E2B thinking-off study](GEMMA_E2B_FRESH_REPEAT_FINDINGS_2026-09-28.md) is a separate configuration. The current thinking-on snapshot has only one full pass under each of two prompts, so the two configurations do not yet support a claim that enabling thinking caused their score difference.
