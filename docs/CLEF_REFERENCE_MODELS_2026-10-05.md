# Decision-model references for the Clef workstream, 5 October 2026

The [linked leaderboard](https://clef-evals.workers-ai-mle.workers.dev/) is useful for finding typed-decision model families and task categories. Its September 28 Decision Index snapshot covers 38 scored benchmarks grouped into Knowledge & Reasoning, Language Understanding, Retrieval & Classification, Tools & Automation, and Arts & Human Taste. The index is chance-corrected and coverage-adjusted; unanswered requests count as wrong. Those categories help with candidate discovery, but they are not the candidate-experience benchmark's rubric.

Treat these results as discovery signals. The page marks Cloudflare's Clef results as author-reported and draws its other rows from the community Decision Index. It warns that Cloudflare latency uses different hardware from the community measurements. They come from separate suites, prompts, scoring, and hardware; reported latency and performance do not predict results on our 60 feedback reviews or our execution setup. None is a result in this repository. The linked page is updated over time and includes marked self-reported entries, so use the dated September 28 snapshot for this shortlist. The page and its [reproduction/scoring kit](https://github.com/apolinario/decision-index) describe the source benchmark and its limits.

The exact families above are absent from the current executed and pending rows reviewed in the [MVP roster](MVP_ROSTER_ACCOUNTING.md) and [remaining roster](REMAINING_ROSTER_2026-09-29.md). Some share a base architecture with other experiments; that does not make them the same model, interface, or configuration. This is a shortlist, not an expansion of the roster. Before any candidate enters it, verify one exact route and model revision, request/output compatibility with the existing 60-record protocol, availability and price, and a separate reviewed admission. Hosted-route status, account access, current prices, and quotas were not checked in this research; no weights were downloaded and no model calls were made.
