Class counts matter here. The reference set has 31 negative sentiment records, 11 positive, 8 mixed, 8 neutral, and 2 insufficient-information cases. Of the eight sentiment errors, three positive records were called mixed, and four of the eight neutral records received a different class. For serious concerns, one of 25 reference `yes` cases was called `insufficient_information`; one reference `no` was called `yes`; three of six reference `insufficient_information` cases were called `no`. The feed retains the full per-field confusion counts.

The native response includes both a selected-choice probability distribution and a separate `confidence` value. They are not interchangeable in the saved responses. The feed bins the reported confidence and counts correct and wrong field judgments at explicit thresholds of 0.5, 0.7, and 0.9. At 0.7, the first pass covers 30 sentiment judgments with no label mismatch, 4 follow-up judgments with none, 19 serious-concern judgments with one, and 53 testimonial judgments with one. These are descriptive counts on the development set. No abstention was executed, and the values have not been calibrated as probabilities of correctness.

Timing is measured by the client around each request and includes network and local work. It is not provider inference duration.
