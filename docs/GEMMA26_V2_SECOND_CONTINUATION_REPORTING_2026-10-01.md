The [second continuation controller](../scripts/gemma26_v2_second_continuation.py) preserves two separate failed requests. `DEV-007` belongs to the original fresh1/P2 interruption. `DEV-002` belongs to the later fresh2/P0 interruption and retains its $0.01974272 unknown-cost upper bound. Neither interrupted dispatch becomes a clean 60-comment pass.

The [new reporter](../scripts/build_gemma26_second_continuation_findings.py) checks the frozen second manifest and the first public report. While the 58-position `DEV-003` to `DEV-060` suffix is absent or unfinished, it reports an incomplete, unscored stage and refuses to write a public final. A final fixed-60 fresh2/P0 tally is possible only after the suffix closes in order. That tally combines the original `DEV-001` result, the retained `DEV-002` service error, and 58 separately dispatched results. It uses provisional reference labels and marks the phase as a composite interrupted run, not a clean matched repeat.

After a stage closes, the reporter's `--export-closed-stage` operation first invokes the controller's strict closure check. It then creates an exclusive public summary containing only record IDs, request hashes, structured judgments, numeric token counts, client durations, and known charges. The summary stores hashes of the claim, review, journal, private attempts, responses, and wire capture. A separate `stage.public-review.json` must approve the exact summary SHA-256 after manual privacy inspection; without it, the stage remains unscored for publication.

A public archive can rebuild the closed report with the approved summary, claim, review, journal and frozen plans, without the new stage's private attempt, response or wire files. The reporter checks exact request order and hashes, journal completion and settled costs, response shape, and the summary's source-hash attestation. When the private files are also available, it additionally reparses and compares their bytes and classifications. The public-only check relies on the controller's export-time closure verification and the manual summary review; it cannot independently reconstruct absent private provider bytes. The feed contains no raw provider bodies, error text, account identifiers, quota headers, credentials, or moving budget ledger. Client duration includes transport and local recording work; it is not model inference time. Missing provider token counts remain missing.

This is an offline reporting tool. Its [tests](../tests/test_build_gemma26_second_continuation_findings.py) cover the incomplete cutoff, frozen source changes, public field allowlisting, exact 60-position scoring, refusal to export an unclosed stage, and rebuilding a closed report from a relocated archive without the new private stage files. No public report was exported here.
