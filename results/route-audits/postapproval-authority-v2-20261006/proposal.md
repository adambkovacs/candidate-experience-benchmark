The v2 module reconstructs the original hold source digest from the reviewed manifest, budget manifest, partition ID, child path and amount. It checks all 60 attempt IDs and settlements, then verifies the exact master events. It releases only the child reconciliation's unused amount; any unknown charge stays accounted.

The [v2 module](../../../scripts/postapproval_authority_v2.py) retains the original header and all historical hold bytes. It locks the authority inode, verifies the audited original prefix, validates each prior release and its sources, checks the current head and cap, then appends one durable event. A partial final line after a crash fails closed and requires manual recovery; the module does not truncate history. Its separate `hold_authority` uses net accounting under the same lock. Its read-only `verify_joint_headroom` checks exact authority and master heads, the $12.38 master cap, unresolved charges, active partitions and available capacity under locks. That preflight is a snapshot, not an allocation or dispatch right.

The [offline tests](../../../tests/test_postapproval_authority_v2.py) use temporary authority copies. They cover original history preservation, the exact Jev release, a subsequent mixed-provider hold, stale and duplicate heads, excessive release, wrong stage/child/master, changed evidence, crash tails, simultaneous holds, and a separate two-record closed suffix with known cost retained. The generic suffix fixture follows the same reviewed receipt, completion, child and master binding contract; no future suffix has been released. The tests also call the old [native hold writer](../../../scripts/openrouter_native_variants_v2.py) on a v2 copy and confirm it rejects the new event without appending.
